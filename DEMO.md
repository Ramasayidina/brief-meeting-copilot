# Demo script — Brief, Meeting Co-pilot (≈ 2 min 30 s)

**Setup.** `npm run build` + `npm run preview` (or `npm run dev`), open the
page in a browser at 100% zoom. The glasses view is on the left; the
"phone side" panel on the right shows every write action the moment it
happens — keep both visible while recording. The demo day is pinned to
09:48, so every take is identical.

**The one-line pitch (say first, over the glance screen):**
"Brief is a meeting co-pilot for the Even Realities G2: it briefs you before
every meeting — and lets you act on it, hands-free, in one tap."

---

### Beat 1 · The glance (0:00–0:20)
The glasses show: **"NEXT · in 12 min — Q3 Roadmap Review"**, who's in it,
and "Follow-up ready: Budget sync".
*Say:* "I'm walking to my next meeting. One glance tells me what it is, when,
and who's coming — no phone out of my pocket."

### Beat 2 · The brief (0:20–0:50)
**Tap** → Brief page 1: why the meeting matters ("Ana moved the launch to
Dec 12 and needs your scope sign-off today") and 3 talking points.
**Swipe ↓** → page 2: attendees, the latest email-thread summary, open items,
and "Needs from you".
*Say:* "One tap, and I know why this meeting exists, what to say, and what
they need from me — written by the AI from my calendar and email threads."

### Beat 3 · Read **and write**: running late (0:50–1:25)
**Tap** → Act menu. **Tap** "Running late — send note" → the glasses show the
AI-drafted note and who receives it. **Tap SEND**.
*Say:* "I'm stuck in the lift. The AI drafts the note; I just confirm."
Point at the phone panel: the message appears in the write log — in
production this leaves via Gmail/Chat in the same second.

### Beat 4 · Ask the AI (1:25–1:55)
Back on the glance screen, **hold** (long-press) → Ask Brief.
Pick **"What do they need from me?"** → the answer appears: the exact
decision the room is waiting for.
*Say:* "Holding the temple asks the AI. No wake-word roulette, no phone —
and in production this is voice, through the G2's own mics."

### Beat 5 · Approve the follow-up (1:55–2:20)
Glance → **Tap** → **Tap** (Act) → scroll to **"Approve follow-up email"** →
review the AI draft of this morning's Budget sync follow-up → **Tap SEND**.
The follow-up email lands in the phone-side log.
*Say:* "Brief even clears my admin: the follow-up from my last meeting was
drafted before I asked. Review on the glasses, send in one tap."

### Close (2:20–2:30)
**Double-tap** back to the glance.
*Say:* "Read and write, in seconds, without touching a phone. That's Brief —
built on the Even Hub SDK, ready for Google Calendar, Gmail and Claude the
moment it ships."

---

**Recording tips**
- Click the on-screen buttons (Tap / Swipe ↓ / Hold) rather than the lens —
  the gestures read more clearly on video.
- Keep each screen on camera ~3 seconds; the app auto-returns home after a
  send, so don't rush the confirmation screens.
- If a take goes wrong, reload the page — the demo state resets completely.
